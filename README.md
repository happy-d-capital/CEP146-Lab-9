## Part 1:

Prompt 1: "Fix my laptop."
Problem: Too vague
better prompt: fix the audio issue, on my laptop that runs arch linux, that happens when I connect two headphones at the same time.

Prompt 2: "Who will win the Stanley Cup next season?"
Problem: Ignoring AI limitations
better prompt: who is more likely to win the Stanley cup next season in your opinion?

Prompt 3: "Why won't it open?"
Problem: Assuming the AI knows your context
better prompt: why my suitcase won't open when I pull the zipper?

Prompt 4: "Tell me about healthy breakfasts." (The person wanted a quick list to take shopping, but got a long essay.)
Problem: Output format not specified
better prompt: Tell me what to buy in a store for a healthy breakfasts.
